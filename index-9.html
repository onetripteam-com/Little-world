TO: DOCTREEFROM: SOMEONE WHO OWED YOU A LITTLE NOTE

Dear Doctree, 💌

Doctree, I used to write you small letters and random notes, remember? I haven't done that in a while. So here's one again. Only this time the note somehow became a whole website. Don't ask me how. 😭

I just wanted to say this properly: you are important to me, and I am really thankful for everything. For the little things, the laughs, the conversations and simply for being you. You may not realise it, but you make a difference.

Also, please don't forget how much you deserve: lovely days, people who listen, small surprises, nice jhumkas, peaceful evenings, and the freedom to be completely yourself. You don't have to earn kindness by doing something for others. You deserve it as you are.

And yes, I know you're not feeling your best right now. Get well soon, okay? Rest properly. But this page isn't only for today. Open it on a random Tuesday too, when you want a little smile. I'm glad you're here, Doctree.

Your slightly annoying friend ✿
P.S. You deserve good days. This website is just the trailer.
